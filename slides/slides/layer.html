Backtest: 56 assets, May 2019 – Oct 2026, out-of-sample forecasts only. Growth is about the same: +579% vs. +568%.

The model forecasts; the decision layer acts. Every day it asks one question: is this week’s market forecast busier than 90% of all earlier days? If yes, the mood is Stormy and every asset is held at half; otherwise everything stays fully invested. It only compares with the past, and trades at the next day’s close after costs. Because it only steps in during the stormiest weeks, about 4% of days, it keeps the growth of buy and hold but takes much smaller falls: in the COVID crash −21% instead of −34%, and a higher return per unit of risk overall, 1.37 versus 1.22. The 90% line isn’t tuned; it’s the same Stormy band the app shows.
