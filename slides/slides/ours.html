Blue = ours. log() on the five volatility inputs: the COVID shock drops from 11.7σ to 4.3σ outside the training data. The decision layer acts on the forecast: +579% vs. +568% buy and hold, COVID drop −21% vs. −34%.

Two additions to the paper’s model, both in blue. First, at the inputs: the five volatility inputs are log-scaled before standardising, which keeps a crash close to the range the model learned from. Second, on top: the decision layer. It turns the forecast into an action, holding every asset at half when the market mood is Stormy, meaning this week’s forecast is busier than 90% of earlier days. That layer is where the portfolio results come from: about the same growth as buy and hold, a higher return per unit of risk (1.37 vs. 1.22), and a much smaller fall in the COVID crash, −21% instead of −34%. Hyperparameters follow the authors’ code: width 16, 3,177 weights, retrains in 12 minutes on a laptop.
