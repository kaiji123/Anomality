The trained model and its forecasts feed the live app. Middle lane: the regime service turns forecasts into the mood and the expected move, the decision layer turns the mood into a position, and the FastAPI backend serves it all to the web app. Bottom: the classic per-asset model we started with, kept as a baseline.
