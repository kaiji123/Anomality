In a crash, the original gate stops working

Raw inputs (original)

The crash looks 11.7 standard deviations off the charts. The gate extrapolates and herds 93% of stocks into one expert, doing worse than a naive forecast.

Log inputs (ours)

The same crash is 4.3 standard deviations out. The gate stays in familiar range, keeps judging stocks one by one, and beats the naive forecast.

The fix is one line of maths: take the log of the volatility inputs before standardising. A shock of 102% versus a normal 20% is a five-times jump on a raw scale but a modest step on a log scale, so the gate never sees numbers wildly outside what it learned.
