The product: a weekly report anyone can read

Market mood

Calm, Normal, Choppy or Stormy, compared with seven years of history.

Moves in dollars

“About ±$10 this week” for every asset, not annualized volatility.

Surprise flags

Weeks that moved twice as much as forecast, plus the classic anomaly log.

The app turns every forecast into plain language: the mood, the expected move in percent and in dollars, and the weeks that surprised the model. Open any asset to see its own forecast against what actually happened since 2019, and compare it with the original research model.
