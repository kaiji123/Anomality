Stormy = this week’s market forecast is busier than 90% of earlier days. Half invested on only 4% of days. No training, no tuning; 0.05% costs, trades the next day.

Backtest on out-of-sample forecasts only. Reward / risk is the Sharpe ratio. Not financial advice.

The decision layer asks one question a day: is the market mood Stormy? If yes, every asset is held at half; otherwise fully invested. It ends up with about the same growth as buy and hold, but smaller drops: in the COVID crash it fell 21% at worst instead of 34%.
